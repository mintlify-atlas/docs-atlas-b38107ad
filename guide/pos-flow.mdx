The 🛒 **Cobrar** tab is the heart of Allipos. Instead of a blank screen or open number pad, every sale is broken into exactly **4 steps**, each occupying its own full-screen view so the child cashier never has to hold more than one decision in their head at a time.

## Progress bar

At the top of every step a progress bar tracks how far along the current sale is.

```text
Paso 1 de 4  ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  25%
Paso 2 de 4  ████░░░░░░░░░░░░  50%
Paso 3 de 4  ████████░░░░░░░░  75%
Paso 4 de 4  ████████████████ 100%
```

The label **"Paso X de 4"** appears beside the bar so even early readers always know where they stand.

## The 4-step checkout flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1 — Select Product">
    Allipos opens directly on the product grid — no menu, no login. Every product in the catalog appears as a large card showing:

    - A **photo** of the item
    - The **product name** in bold
    - The **unit price** (e.g. `$18.00`)

    The cashier simply **taps the card** for the item being sold. The card highlights on selection and the app immediately advances to Step 2.

    <Note>
      If the catalog is empty, this step shows a friendly prompt instead of the grid:
      *"No hay productos cargados — ve a la pestaña **PRODUCTOS** para agregar el primer producto."*
      See [Manage your product catalog](/guide/products) to add items before running your first sale.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2 — Set Quantity">
    After selecting a product, the cashier sets how many units the customer is buying.

    The screen shows:
    - A large image of the selected product
    - The unit price as a reminder (e.g. `$18.00 c/u`)
    - Oversized **`−`** and **`+`** buttons that decrement / increment the quantity counter
    - A live **TOTAL A COBRAR** banner that multiplies `price × quantity` in real time:

    ```text
    ┌──────────────────────────────┐
    │      TOTAL A COBRAR          │
    │          $36.00              │
    └──────────────────────────────┘
    ```

    The **`−`** button is disabled when quantity is `1` to prevent accidentally going below one item.

    At the bottom, two navigation buttons appear:
    - **⬅️ Volver** — returns to the product grid (Step 1)
    - **Cobrar $XX.XX ➡️** — advances to payment entry (Step 3)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3 — Accept Payment">
    This step gives the cashier two ways to enter the amount received from the customer — they can use either method or combine both:

    #### Option A — Tap coin / bill buttons

    A visual grid of **7 denomination buttons** displays real photographs of Mexican currency in circulation:

    | Denomination | Type     |
    |:-------------|:---------|
    | $1           | Moneda   |
    | $2           | Moneda   |
    | $5           | Moneda   |
    | $10          | Moneda   |
    | $20          | Billete  |
    | $50          | Billete  |
    | $100         | Billete  |

    Every tap **accumulates** the running total shown in the numeric input field above. The voice assistant immediately announces each denomination tapped aloud in Spanish (e.g. *"veinte pesos"*).

    #### Option B — Type directly

    A numeric input field (`inputmode="decimal"`) lets the cashier type any arbitrary peso amount directly — useful for exact or mixed amounts.

    #### Smart shortcut buttons

    - **✨ ¡PAGO EXACTO! ($XX.XX)** — sets the received amount to exactly the total payable in one tap, triggering the voice confirmation *"Pago exacto"*
    - **🧹 Borrar** — clears the input back to zero and announces *"Borrado"*

    #### Real-time validation

    The app checks the entered amount against the sale total on every change:

    - If `amount < total`, a **red alert** appears:
      > ⚠️ Recibido ($X.XX) < Total ($X.XX). Faltan $X.XX.

    - If `amount ≥ total`, a **green confirmation** replaces it:
      > ✅ Dinero suficiente • Cambio a entregar: $X.XX

    The **"Calcular Cambio ➡️"** button remains **disabled** until the entered amount meets or exceeds the total payable. Once sufficient, the button activates and a tap on it plays the success fanfare, advances to Step 4, and triggers the voice narration of the change.

    <Note>
      Voice narration uses the Web Speech API with locale `es-MX`. See [Voice & Audio](/features/voice-audio) for browser compatibility details and customization options.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4 — Change & Success">
    The final screen celebrates the completed sale and tells the cashier exactly what change to hand back.

    #### Change amount

    The change due is displayed in **large green text** at the top of the result card:

    ```text
    CAMBIO A ENTREGAR AL CLIENTE
           $14.00
    ```

    If the customer paid the exact amount, the screen shows *"✨ ¡Pago exacto! No hay cambio que entregar."* instead of a breakdown.

    #### Visual coin/bill breakdown

    When change is owed, Allipos calculates the optimal combination of denominations and shows each piece as a card with:
    - A **real photo** of the coin or bill
    - The **quantity** to hand back (e.g. `2x`)
    - The denomination label (e.g. `Billete $10`)

    #### Alternative combinations

    If the cashbox is missing a particular denomination, the cashier can cycle through up to **6 mathematically valid combinations** using:

    > **🔄 Ver otra forma de dar cambio** `1/3`

    The counter (e.g. `1/3`) shows which option is currently displayed and how many alternatives exist. Each time the button is tapped, the new combination is also read aloud by the voice assistant.

    #### Replay narration

    The **🔊 Escuchar** button replays the full voice narration at any time — ideal when a denomination is hard to identify or when the surrounding environment is noisy.

    #### Completing the sale

    Tapping **✅ CONCLUIR VENTA Y SIGUIENTE** does the following:

    1. Calls `Store.addSale()` which writes the transaction record to LocalStorage under the `allipos_sales` key (see [Data Model](/reference/data-model))
    2. Adds the `totalPayable` amount to the running `totalSales` accumulator
    3. Prepends the new record to the `history` array (newest first)
    4. Resets all internal state (`selectedProduct`, `quantity`, `selectedBill`, `currentComboIndex`)
    5. Returns to **Step 1** so the cashier is immediately ready for the next customer

    <Note>
      Voice narration in Spanish (`es-MX`) automatically reads denominations in Step 3 and the full change instruction in Step 4. Learn more in [Voice & Audio](/features/voice-audio).
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets saved when you tap "CONCLUIR VENTA"

Each completed sale is persisted to LocalStorage by `Store.addSale()`. The method stamps a unique `id` (e.g. `sale_1700000000100`) and an ISO 8601 `timestamp` before writing the record:

```js
Store.addSale({
  productId:      selectedProduct.id,
  productName:    selectedProduct.name,
  quantity:       quantity,
  totalPayable:   totalPayable,   // price × quantity
  amountPaid:     selectedBill,   // cash received
  changeReturned: change          // amountPaid − totalPayable
});
```

The complete shape of the persisted object is documented in [Data Model](/reference/data-model). To view all saved sales and reset the register, see [Sales Summary](/guide/sales-summary).
